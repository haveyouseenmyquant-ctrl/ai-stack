Summary

Treatment plan — nothing here runs until you decide

Before you turn it on

Laptop Doctor will, on its own:
  • check this computer once a week, on mains power and while you're away
  • free up local copies of cloud files that are already safely synced (they download again when opened)
  • compress cold files in place with Windows' own compression (they still open normally)
  • zip only the folders you pick here, after checking every file in the zip
It will never, without you:
  • delete, move, quit or disable anything: that goes into a plan you approve or decline
  • touch system files, other users' files, passwords, mail, browser profiles, git repositories, or anything changed in the last 7 days
  • turn off Defender, the firewall or BitLocker
Everything it does is in History, with Undo. The Claude summary never gets the names of your files or folders.
